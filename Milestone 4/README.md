# Project Overview

The **Agentic AI for Franchise Management System with Performance Monitoring Assistance** is an intelligent business-support application developed to help franchise owners, managers and administrators understand the performance of their franchise network.

The system brings together operational analytics, machine-learning models, AI-assisted decision support, document analysis, multilingual translation and administrative controls within a single platform.

Instead of requiring managers to analyse different datasets separately, the application provides dedicated intelligence modules for important franchise functions such as:

* Employee and workforce performance
* Outlet revenue and performance
* Inventory and stock management
* Marketing campaigns
* Customer feedback and sentiment
* Audit and compliance
* Executive-level performance monitoring
* SOP translation
* Franchise document analysis

The platform is implemented using **Python and Streamlit**, with a SQLite database for application data and machine-learning and language models for intelligent analysis.

---

# Problem Statement

Managing multiple franchise outlets involves handling a large amount of operational information.

Managers need to continuously monitor:

* Revenue generated by different outlets
* Employee performance and retention risks
* Inventory availability
* Marketing campaign effectiveness
* Customer satisfaction
* Compliance and audit results
* Operational alerts

Traditional dashboards generally present numerical information and historical reports. However, managers may still need to manually interpret the data to understand the reason behind a performance issue and decide what action should be taken.

This project addresses that gap by introducing an **agent-based AI management system** where different intelligence modules focus on different business functions.

The system combines structured analytics, machine-learning predictions and AI-generated assistance to provide a more interactive approach to franchise performance monitoring.

---

# Our Approach

The application follows a modular architecture in which operational information is first collected from the database and then processed by specialised intelligence modules.

Each module performs a specific business task.

For example:

* Workforce intelligence analyses employee-related information.
* Outlet intelligence evaluates revenue and profitability.
* Inventory intelligence identifies stock-related risks.
* Marketing intelligence studies campaign performance.
* Sentiment intelligence analyses customer feedback.
* Audit intelligence evaluates compliance.
* Executive intelligence combines important KPIs from the entire network.
* Translation intelligence handles multilingual SOP content.
* Document intelligence retrieves information from uploaded franchise documents.

The AI Copilot can then use the available business context to assist users with questions and provide decision-support responses.

The AI Copilot acts as the conversational intelligence component of the system.

* How are marketing campaigns performing?

The Copilot uses available application context to provide more relevant responses.

The purpose of the Copilot is to support decision-making rather than replace human management decisions.

# Limitations

### 1. Demonstration Data

The project currently works with sample/generated franchise information for development and demonstration.

### 2. SQLite Database

SQLite is suitable for a prototype and smaller deployments, but a large-scale enterprise deployment would require a production database.

### 3. Local Model Processing

AI and translation performance depends on the available hardware.

### 4. Document Retrieval

The current document retrieval mechanism is lightweight. A production version could use a persistent vector database and embedding-based retrieval.

### 5. Prototype Deployment

The current system is primarily designed around Streamlit and local/Google Colab execution.
